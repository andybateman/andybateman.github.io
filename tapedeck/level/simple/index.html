Tape Deck in Simple

Simple is Tape Deck made easy: the cassette, your music and the few settings you need, with everything else put away. It plays one clean side, with keys that do what they say.

If you have Tape Deck

On an iPhone, iPad or Mac with Tape Deck, this link opens it in Simple by itself, with tape settings showing the switch. If you're reading this page instead:

  1. Open Tape Deck.
  2. Tap the gear at the top of the screen (on a Mac, Tape Deck › Settings…).
  3. At the top of tape settings, choose Simple.

Every setting comes back with Advanced, at the same place, whenever you want it.

If you don't have it yet

Tape Deck is in beta on TestFlight for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Open the link on the device you want it on, install TestFlight from the App Store if it asks, then choose Accept and Install. It starts in Simple.
