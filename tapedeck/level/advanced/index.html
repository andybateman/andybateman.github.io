Tape Deck in Advanced

Advanced shows every setting Tape Deck has: the sides and the leader, the tape's sound and wear, the two sets of keys, the label and its looks.

If you have Tape Deck

On an iPhone, iPad or Mac with Tape Deck, this link opens it in Advanced by itself, with tape settings showing the switch. If you're reading this page instead:

  1. Open Tape Deck.
  2. Tap the gear at the top of the screen (on a Mac, Tape Deck › Settings…).
  3. At the top of tape settings, choose Advanced.

Simple, at the same place, puts the extra settings away again.

If you don't have it yet

Tape Deck is in beta on TestFlight for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Open the link on the device you want it on, install TestFlight from the App Store if it asks, then choose Accept and Install. It starts in Simple.
