Tape Deck Features Help

Privacy

Tape Deck collects nothing about you. This page says what it does keep, and where.

What is collected

Nothing. Tape Deck has no account, no sign-in of its own, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. No information about you or how you use the app is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your devices

  • Your music, books and settings are kept on the device: the albums you copy in or download, your place in each tape, and how you've set the deck up.
  • Your favourites go between your own devices through your iCloud account, in iCloud's own storage for the app. The developer can't see them.
  • Music in iCloud Drive, in the Tape Deck folder, is your own iCloud Drive, read by the app on each of your devices.
  • The Apple Watch app talks only to your iPhone, to show what's in its deck.
  • Your Apple Music library, if you use Apple Music mode, is listed on the device so it opens at once; the covers are kept with it. Nothing from Apple Music is downloaded or kept as audio.

Servers you choose

Tape Deck connects to nothing until you give it an address. If you enter a music server or an Audiobookshelf server, the app talks to that server alone, to list and play what's on it and, for books and podcasts, to keep your place there. Those servers are yours, or run by someone you choose, and their own policies apply. The address, the Audiobookshelf key and the server's sign-in are kept on the device; a server's password is passed to it once and not kept.

Permissions

Only for what you switch on. To use Apple Music, asked the first time you choose Apple Music from the source button, so the app can list and play your Apple Music library; nothing about what you play there reaches the developer, and Apple's own terms cover Apple Music. To use your media library, asked when you turn on Music on this iPhone, so the app can play the songs synced to it; they stay on the device. And the motion sensor, asked on an iPhone when you turn on the exploded view, so the cassette can tilt with the phone; nothing about it leaves the page. The app asks for nothing else: not your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Children

As nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children.

Changes and questions

If this ever changes, this page will say so first, with the date. Questions: tapedeck@andybateman.com.
